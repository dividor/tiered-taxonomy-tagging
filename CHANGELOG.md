# Changelog

Notable changes to this repository. Dates are when the change reached `main`.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11)

The code, notebooks, results and report behind the [blog post](https://medium.com/@astrobagel/tagging-documents-with-a-tiered-taxonomy-e86b73d602de).

- 23 methods for tagging documents with a tiered taxonomy, scored on MultiEURLEX and on HumSet 1.0.0 and 2.0.0.
- A combined method that weighs several trained models' suggestions, compared with the published BERT
  (MultiEURLEX) and HumBERT (HumSet) benchmarks on their full test sets.
- One command runs everything: pinned, checked downloads, the notebooks, the GPU runs on Hugging Face Jobs, and
  the report.
